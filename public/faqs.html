FAQs

Everything you need to know before you order.

Home / FAQs
How do I order online?
Head to the Order page, choose the Lantana location, add your treats to the cart, and check out securely with Square. You'll get a pickup confirmation when your order is placed.
Is this pickup or delivery?
All online orders are for in-store pickup at our Lantana shop during open hours (Tue–Sat 11am–7pm). For large or custom orders, ask us about other arrangements.
How fresh are the treats?
Everything is baked fresh daily. Online ordering lets us box your treats and have them ready when you arrive.
Do you take custom orders?
Absolutely! Visit our Custom Orders page. Please give us at least 48 hours' notice (3–5 days for large orders of 50+ pieces).
Do your treats contain allergens?
Our kitchen handles wheat, dairy, eggs, nuts and soy. Items may contain or come into contact with these allergens. If you have a serious allergy, please contact us before ordering.
How will I know my order went through?
After paying with Square you'll see a confirmation page, and the shop receives the order instantly in the Square dashboard and app. We'll have it ready for pickup.
What payment methods do you accept?
Online checkout is powered by Square and accepts all major credit and debit cards. In-store we accept cards and cash.
Can I change or cancel an order?
Need to make a change? Contact us as soon as possible via the Contact page and we'll do our best to help.

Baked with heart

Blissri began with a simple belief: that a good day deserves something warm, sweet and made by hand. What started as baking for friends and family grew into the cosy little bakeshoppe in Lantana you know today.

We still do things the slow way — real butter, real vanilla, and recipes tested until they're just right. Nothing leaves our kitchen unless we'd happily serve it to our own family.

  • 🌾Honest ingredients. No preservatives, no shortcuts — just the good stuff.
  • 👐Handmade daily. Every cookie and cupcake is made fresh each morning.
  • 💛Made for community. Birthdays, gifts, or an ordinary Tuesday — we bake for all of it.
